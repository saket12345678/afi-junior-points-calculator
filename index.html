How the points are made

  1. Pick the senior event it grows into80m Hurdles uses the hurdles table, Kids Javelin uses Javelin, Triathlon and Pentathlon use the Decathlon (boys) or Heptathlon (girls) table.
  2. Take the World Athletics curveSame shape seniors use: better marks earn points faster near the top.
  3. Stretch it to the recordThe curve is rescaled so the Indian National Record of the category lands on exactly 1000.
  4. Score the markRunning: a × (b − time)². Jumps and throws: a × (mark + b)² + c. Jumps use centimetres.
  5. Round downPoints are always rounded down to a whole number, like World Athletics does.
Covers every individual event on the AFI programme for U14 (Triathlon A, B, C and Kids Javelin), U16 (60m, 600m, 80m Hurdles, High Jump, Long Jump, Shot Put, Javelin, Pentathlon) and U18 (12 events with youth implements). National Records are as of 28 October 2026, including those set at the 41st National Junior Championships. When a record changes, type the new mark in "Record worth 1000 points" and every score re-anchors. U18 scores are for comparison. Official U18 and U20 awards use World Athletics points.
