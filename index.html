How the points are made

  1. Pick the senior event it grows into80m Hurdles uses the hurdles table, Kids Javelin uses Javelin, Triathlon and Pentathlon use the Decathlon (boys) or Heptathlon (girls) table.
  2. Take the World Athletics curveSame shape seniors use: better marks earn points faster near the top.
  3. Stretch it to our NRWe rescale the curve so the Indian National Record of this category lands on exactly 1000.
  4. Score the markRunning: a × (b − time)². Jumps and throws: a × (mark + b)² + c. Jumps use centimetres.
  5. Round downPoints are always rounded down to a whole number, like World Athletics does.
Records used are the pre-meet National Records printed for the 41st National Junior Athletics Championships 2026. When a record changes, update the nr value for that event and the curve re-anchors automatically. U14 and U16 use these points for Best Athlete. U18 values are shown for analysis; official U18 and U20 awards use World Athletics points on senior equipment.
