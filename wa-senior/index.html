Rules applied

Result score

  • Points come from the World Athletics Scoring Tables 2025, from 1 to 1400.
  • A mark better than the 1400 line shows as 1400+. A mark below the 1-point line scores 0.
  • Road races and road walks are scored to whole seconds. Track times use hundredths.
  • Short track ("sh") events have their own tables and no wind.

Wind

  • Applies to 100m, 200m, 100mH, 110mH, Long Jump and Triple Jump.
  • 1 m/s of wind is worth 6 points, so 0.6 points for every 0.1 m/s.
  • Tailwind from 0 to +2.0 m/s: no change. Above +2.0 m/s: minus 6 points per m/s of the full reading.
  • Headwind: plus 6 points per m/s.
  • No wind reading: minus 30 points.

Placing score

  • Added to the result score to give the performance score.
  • Given in the final. OW, DF, GW and GL also give it in the round before the final.
  • 5000m and 3000mSC groups and 10,000m have their own tables. The 10km road race has its own table.
  • A National Senior Outdoor Championships is category B. Only one per country per year gives placing scores.

Not counted by WA

  • Results with youth or junior hurdles or implements.
  • Intermediate splits.
  • Results not ratified or not held under World Athletics rules.
  • Placing scores for combined events, relays, walks and road races (other than 10km) are in other sections of the WA rules and are not included here.
